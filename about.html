About EcoTrack

Promoting sustainability through everyday actions

Our Mission

EcoTrack is designed to help students develop eco-friendly habits by making sustainability simple, fun, and trackable. We provide a dashboard that encourages daily green actions—from recycling to energy saving.

Why It Matters

Every small action counts. EcoTrack motivates students to adopt habits that protect the environment, reduce waste, and build a cleaner future. By tracking progress, learners stay engaged and responsible.

What You Can Do

  • Track your daily eco actions
  • Learn practical sustainability tips
  • Build consistent habits that make a difference
