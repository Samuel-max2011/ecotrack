Daily Eco Tips

Turn off taps while brushing

Save up to 8 gallons of water per day by turning off the tap while brushing your teeth.

Fix leaking faucets

A single dripping faucet can waste over 3,000 gallons of water annually. Fix leaks promptly.

Use a bucket instead of a hose

Using a bucket for washing can save hundreds of gallons of water compared to a running hose.

Switch to LED bulbs

LED bulbs use up to 75% less energy and last 25 times longer than incandescent lighting.

Unplug electronics when not in use

Electronics in standby mode still consume energy. Unplugging them can save electricity.

Use energy-efficient appliances

Energy Star-rated appliances can significantly reduce your energy bills and carbon footprint.

Recycle paper, plastic, and glass

Recycling reduces waste in landfills and conserves natural resources for future use.

Compost your food waste

Composting turns organic waste into nutrient-rich soil, reducing methane emissions from landfills.

Use reusable shopping bags

Reusable bags reduce the need for single-use plastics, which can take centuries to decompose.

Carpool to reduce emissions

Sharing rides with others reduces the number of vehicles on the road, cutting down on greenhouse gas emissions.

Use public transportation

Buses and trains are more energy-efficient than individual cars, helping to lower your carbon footprint.

Walk or bike for short distances

Walking or biking not only saves fuel but also promotes a healthier lifestyle.

Maintain your vehicle for efficiency

Regular maintenance ensures your vehicle runs efficiently, reducing fuel consumption and emissions.

Consider electric or hybrid vehicles

Electric and hybrid vehicles produce fewer emissions and can save money on fuel in the long run.

Buy organic produce

Organic farming avoids harmful pesticides, promoting healthier ecosystems and food.

Reduce meat consumption

Meat production is resource-intensive. Eating less meat can lower your environmental impact.

Support local farmers

Buying locally reduces transportation emissions and supports your community's economy.

Plan meals to avoid food waste

Meal planning helps you buy only what you need, reducing food waste and saving money.

Use reusable containers for storage

Reusable containers reduce the need for single-use plastics and keep food fresh longer.
